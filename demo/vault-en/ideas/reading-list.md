# To read later

## Persistent data structures

A structure that returns a new version instead of altering the old one. It is
what makes undo free, and the idea behind most immutable state libraries.

## Vector databases

Storing meaning rather than words raises a new question: how to find the nearest
neighbour without comparing against everything. HNSW is the answer that stuck.

## Typography on small screens

Almost everything known about leading and line length came from paper. Worth
checking what still holds when the column is 320 pixels wide.

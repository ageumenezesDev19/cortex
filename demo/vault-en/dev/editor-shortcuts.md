# Editor shortcuts

## Moving around

Jumping to a symbol's definition and back without leaving the keyboard is what
saves the most time in a large file.

## Editing in several places

Selecting every occurrence of a word and editing all three at once avoids the
classic mistake: changing two and forgetting the third.

## Searching the whole project

Search by regular expression with an extension filter. That is how to find where
a constant came from when its name is too generic for a plain grep.

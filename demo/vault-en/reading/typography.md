# Typography notes

## Line length

Between 45 and 75 characters. Longer and the eye loses the start of the next
line; shorter and the rhythm breaks every few words.

## Vertical rhythm

Line height around 1.5 for body text, tighter for headings. Large type needs
proportionally less leading, which is why a heading set at the paragraph's ratio
always looks loose.

## Contrast

Grey text on white is a design decision that costs readability. If the copy
matters it gets full contrast — the softness belongs to the labels around it.

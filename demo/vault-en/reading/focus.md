# Focus and interruption

## The cost of switching

An interrupted task does not resume where it stopped. The mind holds a residue
of the previous problem, and for a while you work on both at half capacity.

## Batching

Answering messages twice a day rather than as they arrive removes most of the
switching without making anyone wait in any real sense.

## What actually helped

Writing down the next concrete step before stopping. Coming back to "continue
the refactor" costs ten minutes; coming back to "extract the parser into its own
module" costs nothing.

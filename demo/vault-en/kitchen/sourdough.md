# Sourdough bread

## The starter

Feed it equal weights of flour and water, always at the same hour. A healthy one
doubles in four to six hours — if it takes twelve, it is weak and the loaf will
come out heavy.

## The dough

Thirty minutes of autolyse before adding the salt. The flour takes up the water
on its own in that time, and the dough turns silky with almost no kneading.

## Why mine came out dense

Baking too early. The rise looked finished because the dough had grown, but the
test is different: press it with a finger and watch how slowly the dent fills
back. If it springs straight back, it is not ready.
